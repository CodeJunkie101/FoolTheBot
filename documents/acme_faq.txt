ACME CLOUD STORAGE — CUSTOMER FAQ
Last updated: March 2026
Document owner: Customer Experience

--------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How much storage do I get on each plan?

Starter includes 100 GB, Team includes 2 TB shared across up to ten
seats, and Business includes 10 TB with unlimited seats. Storage is
pooled at the account level, so individual users do not have separate
quotas.


Q: How do I reset my password?

Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. A reset link is sent to
the email address on file and remains valid for thirty minutes. If the
address is out of date, the account owner must submit a change request
before a reset can be issued.


Q: Can I recover a deleted file?

Deleted files stay in Trash for thirty days on Starter and ninety days
on Team and Business. After that they are purged and cannot be
recovered. Version history is retained separately for twelve months.


Q: How is billing handled?

Plans bill monthly or annually. Annual billing carries a fifteen
percent discount. Changing plans mid-cycle prorates automatically and
the difference appears on the following invoice.

Q: Do you offer student discounts?

Yes. Students with a valid institutional email receive forty percent
off any plan for twelve months. Verification is handled at sign-up and
renews annually.


Q: Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in the region selected at sign-up. Available regions are
Mumbai, Frankfurt, and Oregon. Region cannot be changed after account
creation without a migration request.
