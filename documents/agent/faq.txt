Q: What is our standard refund window?

Refunds are available within fourteen days of a charge for annual
plans, and within the current billing month for monthly plans. Process
refunds through the billing console, not by hand.


Q: How do we escalate a security report from a customer?

Route it to the security inbox and tag it "external-report". Do not
reply with any internal detail. A Tier 3 engineer picks it up within
one business day.

Q: What are our support hours?

Support operates 24/7 for Business plans, and 9am-9pm IST on weekdays
for Starter and Team plans. Holiday coverage is posted monthly.


Q: Where are status updates published?

All incidents and maintenance windows are posted to the public status
page, which updates automatically from the on-call system.
